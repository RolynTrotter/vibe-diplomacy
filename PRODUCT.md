# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Researchers and analysts watching AI agents play Diplomacy — evaluating strategic behavior, studying inter-agent negotiation, and running post-mortems on completed matches. Secondary: the builder/hacker checking what the system can do. Typical context: desktop browser after a match, reviewing phase-by-phase. Also reachable on mobile during a live game.

## Product Purpose

A phase-by-phase replay viewer for AI Diplomacy matches. Players commit sealed orders to Git; the viewer bakes them into a navigable record — board map, resolved orders, diplomatic messages, and each power's private notebook. The viewer is not the game; it is the observatory. Success looks like: an analyst can reconstruct exactly what every power was thinking and doing at every moment in a match.

## Brand Personality

Contemplative, authoritative, precise. The armchair general: brandy in hand, studying the map in a well-appointed study. Old-world gravitas rendered in dark UI. Confident without aggression, serious without being cold.

## Anti-references

- **Startup analytics**: metric cards, gradient text, hero-number templates, SaaS-cream surfaces — this is not a product dashboard.
- **Terminal cosplay**: green-on-black, ASCII borders, l33t-hacker aesthetic — the game is sophisticated, not edgy.

## Design Principles

1. **Gravitas over novelty.** The board holds something serious. UI weight and restraint should communicate that weight, not compete with it.
2. **Data, not decoration.** Game state is the content. Chrome recedes; information advances.
3. **Scholar, not soldier.** Analytical distance, not battlefield urgency. The viewer is a library, not a command center.
4. **Earned reveal.** Information is available but not shouted. Trust the analyst to navigate; don't preempt them with callouts.
5. **Mobile-first without sacrificing depth.** Thumb-reachable on a phone mid-game, rich and scannable on desktop for post-mortem work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. 4.5:1 body text contrast, all interactive controls labeled, keyboard navigation for phase stepping. The dark theme carries inherent contrast risk — verify every muted text color, not just primary ink.
