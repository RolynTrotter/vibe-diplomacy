# TURKEY — S1901M Strategy Notebook

## Board State (Opening)
- Centers: ANK, CON, SMY (3)
- Units: F ANK, A CON, A SMY
- Adjustment due: none

## Key Neighbors
- **Russia** (4 ctr): F SEV, A MOS, F STP/SC — controls Black Sea and Sea of Azov
- **Austria** (3 ctr): A BUD, A VIE, F TRI — Adriatic fleet threatening Balkans
- **Italy** (3 ctr): A VEN, F NAP — Adriatic + Mediterranean fleets

## Strategic Concerns
1. **Russia is ahead** (4 centers). Must assess if they push south (UKR/ARM) or north/east.
2. **Black Sea is contested** — Russia has F SEV + F STP/SC. F ANK→BLA is critical.
3. **Sea of Azov threat** — Russia's F SEV can threaten A SMY via ARM.
4. **Balkan opportunity** — A CON→SER pressures Austria; AEG control opens Aegean.

## Opening Plan
- **Primary**: Secure Black Sea (F ANK→BLA), push A CON to ARM or SER
- **Secondary**: A SMY→ARM to contest Russia, or A SMY→AEG for Mediterranean control
- **Key question**: Does Russia push south? If ARM, I must contest strongly.

## Diplomatic Priorities
- **Russia**: Probe southern intentions. Can we agree on ARM? Or contest?
- **Italy**: Signal Mediterranean focus. No Adriatic trouble.
- **Austria**: Probe Balkan intentions. SER is valuable — who wants it?

## Potential Deals
- Russia: "I take ARM, you don't push SEV→ARM. We both get safe flanks."
- Italy: "I won't push ADR. You don't push ALB/ION toward me."
- Austria: "I won't push SER if you don't push BUL."
